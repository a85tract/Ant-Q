### A.4 Software cause of the "64 us unit floor" (intervention result) and the corrected sub-circuit results (2026-09-02 12:00)
Mask sweep on the same 46.8 us unit (tag c3_14q_floordiag, 3 runs each): 13 / 12 / 7 masked channels all gave a
~64-65 us period; only the 14-full-channel image (448 KiB) gave 127 us. The starved datasets (#5 and the two
controls, 12 runs) fit one fixed per-unit period of 64.0 +/- 0.3 us (mean +/- s.d. over the per-run periods = elapsed /
n_units), independent of unit length, image size up to 224 KiB and masked-channel count — a fixed-RATE limit, not a
per-boundary latency. The five-angle RTL/code/data investigation behind this section (fetcher RTL, sequencer/DSP RTL,
PS feeder code, simulation feasibility, data fit; each adversarially re-checked) is filed as
results/unit_floor_rootcause_20260902.md: the DDR->ppbuf fetch of a 1+13 unit is 4-11 us and the sequencer/DSP
boundary ~10 DSP cycles, so the PL cannot produce 64 us. Candidate cause: batch_server's MM2S completion poll used
usleep(5) (mm2s_send), and the [BS-T] probe reports "first DMA done +0.063..0.068 ms" for every batch although a
compact image transfers in <= 16 us — consistent with the non-RT kernel's ~50 us timer slack stretching the sleep
(the syscall's duration itself was not timed on the board). With one MM2S per unit (CE compact images, REPT replay)
that caps the feed at one unit per ~64 us. Intervention: software_c3 6e7888b replaces the sleep by sched_yield in
the completion poll; hardware, image and workload unchanged; deployed 11:50. SUPPORTED CONCLUSION: replacing the
sleep-based poll removed the ~64 us feed limit (table below); the mechanism statement is the consistent explanation,
not a directly timed one.
